Introduction

TORTUGA APPS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how the Easy Draw Anime app ("the App") manages user data and utilizes device permissions.

The App is an Augmented Reality (AR) creative tool designed to help users learn to draw sketches by overlaying templates onto the real world (tracing). Its core functionality requires the CAMERA permission for AR tracing and INTERNET access to load templates and reference images from our servers.

02

Information We Collect

We collect and process two distinct categories of data:

Data Accessed Locally Only 🔒 Device Only

This data is processed exclusively on your device for the App's core AR functionality and is never recorded, stored, or sent to our servers or any third party.

📷
Camera Feed (AR Tracing)
The App uses your device's camera for the Augmented Reality tracing feature. This involves real-time processing of video frames to overlay digital images onto a physical surface. The camera feed is ephemeral and never recorded.
🖼️
User Images / Photos
You may select existing images from your device for custom tracing. The content of these images is processed locally and never transmitted.
✏️
Drawing Data
User interactions within the drawing interface are processed locally on your device only.

03

Permissions Used in the App

CAMERA

Required for core functionality. Allows the App to access the camera feed for real-time Augmented Reality tracing.

INTERNET

Required for content delivery (templates, privacy policy) and to connect to AdMob and Firebase services for ads and data collection.

AD_ID

Required by the AdMob SDK to provide advertising and analytics services.

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android ≤ 12 only

Required for Android 12 and older to allow the user to select existing image files from device storage for custom tracing.

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Android 13+

Required for Android 13 and newer to allow the user to select existing image files (photos and pictures) from device storage for custom tracing.

READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED Android 14+

Required for Android 14 and newer to allow the user to select specific images while limiting the App's access to only the selected files.

Children's Privacy

This App is suitable for users of all ages. The App's content is not directed toward children under 13. Given the use of AdMob and the App's general audience, we take the following steps to comply with relevant children's privacy regulations:

  • We use Google Play Families Policy compliant versions of all SDKs.
  • For users known to be under a certain age (e.g., under 13), we may restrict ad personalization or mark ad requests for "child-directed treatment" to ensure compliance with COPPA and GDPR.
🛡️ All ad requests via AdMob can be configured for child-directed treatment as required by applicable law.
