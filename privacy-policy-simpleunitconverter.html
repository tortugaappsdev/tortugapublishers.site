Introduction

TORTUGA APPS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how the Simple Unit Converter app ("the App") manages user data.

The App is a utility tool that functions offline, with a single exception: it may access the internet only to load the Privacy Policy page when requested by the user.

02

Information We Collect

User-Provided Data

When using the App, you may enter values for unit conversions. These inputs are processed locally on your device and are never sent to our servers or any third party.

Automatically Collected Data

The App does not automatically collect personal or device data. It contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.
