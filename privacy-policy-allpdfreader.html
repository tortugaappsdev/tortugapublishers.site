01

Introduction

TORTUGA APPS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how the All PDF Reader app ("the App") manages user data and utilizes device permissions.

The App is a utility tool designed to read and manage PDF files locally on your device. It does not contain advertisements or analytics, and functions primarily offline — with the sole exception of loading this Privacy Policy page when specifically requested by the user.

📄 All PDF processing happens entirely on your device. Your documents are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted anywhere.
02

Information We Collect

User-Provided Data

The App processes files selected by the user for reading. The content of these files and user interactions within documents (such as scrolling or page navigation) are processed locally on your device and are never sent to our servers or any third party. We do not access or collect the content of your PDF files.

Automatically Collected Data

The App does not automatically collect personal or device data. It contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

03

Permissions Used in the App

The App uses the following Android permissions to deliver its core file reading functionality:

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Required to allow the user to select and open PDF files from their device's storage. Used for devices running Android 12 and older.

MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Required for the App to access and manage (view, open, organize) all PDF files on the device, as is necessary for a comprehensive file reader application.

ℹ️ All file management and reading operations are conducted locally on your device. The App does not send or receive any file or personal data over the internet.
